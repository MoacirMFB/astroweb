# Bennu visualizations

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4857/

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. Data provided by NASA/University of Arizona/CSA/York University/MDA. Visualization by Kel Elkins.

- `bennu-spin.mp4`: `bennu_spin_v3_1080p30.mp4`, cropped to a square, resized to 720 pixels, compressed to H.264 at 24 fps, and played at twice the source speed.
- `bennu-light.mp4`: `bennu_spin_light_1080p30.mp4`, cropped to a square, resized to 720 pixels, and compressed to H.264 at 24 fps.
- JPEG posters are frames extracted from the corresponding optimized videos.

These are NASA visualizations of Bennu based on OSIRIS-REx measurements. They illustrate small-body exploration and do not depict the author's mission simulations. The heliocentric diagram shows a computed, illustrative Lambert transfer to a hypothetical asteroid. Its generator and assumptions are in `scripts/generate-transfer.py`. It is not a specific research mission or a real asteroid ephemeris.
